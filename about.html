About Sewa Academy

A Christian-aligned school in Slota, Athi River, building excellence in every child.

Our Story

Private Christian-aligned education in Machakos County.

At Sewa Academy Kenya, we believe every child has unique abilities, talents, and a God-given purpose. Our goal is to provide every learner with opportunities to acquire the knowledge, skills, and competencies needed to succeed in school and become a responsible, confident, and productive member of society.

We are committed to nurturing well-rounded learners through intellectual, spiritual, social, moral, and physical growth. By promoting discipline, integrity, respect, creativity, compassion, and responsibility, we create an environment where children can discover their potential and develop a lifelong love of learning.

Our teaching approach focuses on quality education, individual growth, practical skills, and the development of strong moral character. We work in partnership with parents and the wider community to support every learner's educational journey.

Our Mission

To provide every pupil with opportunities to acquire the skills and competencies necessary to discover their God-given purpose, achieve personal excellence, and become well-rounded learners grounded in a strong Christian foundation.

Our Vision

To be a leading Christian-based co-educational centre of excellence in competency-based education, producing knowledgeable, skilled, responsible, and morally upright learners prepared for the challenges and opportunities of the future.
