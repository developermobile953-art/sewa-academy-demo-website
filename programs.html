Our Levels

Programs

From early play to junior secondary, each level is designed with care, structure, and purpose.

Play Group

Ages 2 – 4

A warm, nurturing start for early learners with guided play, storytelling, basic numeracy, and social skills in a Christian environment.

Kindergarten

Ages 4 – 6

Foundational learning blending play and structured activity. Focus on literacy, early mathematics, creativity, and character building.

Primary School

Grade 1 – 6

Strong CBC-aligned academics with emphasis on critical thinking, reading, science, ICT basics, and moral development.

Junior Secondary

Grade 7 – 9

Preparation for senior secondary with deeper subject mastery, leadership development, and guided career exploration.

Programs & Extras

Beyond the classroom — quality education, holiday camps, and co-curricular enrichment.

Grades 1 – 7

Quality Education

We focus on strong foundations, curiosity, confidence, and practical skills so every learner can grow and prepare for the next stage of learning.

Learners can review lessons and practice at a pace that suits them. Contact the academy for current schedules, access requirements, and availability for Grades 1–7.

All Ages

School Trips & Educational Tours

Exciting educational trips that take students beyond the classroom, combining learning, exploration, adventure, and fun while building teamwork and confidence.

Students can enjoy visits to museums, wildlife parks, cultural and historical sites, nature trails, farms, and other exciting destinations. These trips provide opportunities to discover new places, experience hands-on learning, make new memories, and connect classroom lessons with the real world.

All Levels

Arts & Sports

Arts, music, drama, and sports programs integrated into the curriculum to nurture talent, teamwork, and healthy confidence.

Creative and physical activities give learners opportunities to explore interests, express ideas, and grow through participation. Ask the academy about current activities and schedules.
